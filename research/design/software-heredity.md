# Software heredity: what closed when the grandchild qualified

*2026-10-10, written the day the count reached three. A discussion
record between the keyholder and the agent, kept because the claims in
it will someday face referees, and the calibrated form worked out here
is the one that survives them. Companion artifacts: the closure ledger
(the count), the genome map and freedom register (the boundary), the
sealed trials (the evidence: trialclaims/r23-codex, r24-claude,
g2-codex). Nothing here moves a root.*

## I. What happened, stated exactly

Three sealed claims now exist, each one a trial whose QUALIFYING verdict
re-earns mechanically from its root:

- **trial-r23-codex** — a tree authored blind by one model family,
  grown from the sealed boundary at root 900b6594, re-earning identity
  under full 37-module substitution, the held-out battery, the
  bootstrap, the recursive step, and crosscheck through its own CLI.
- **trial-r24-claude** — the same, from the other family.
- **trial-g2-codex** — the grandchild: a complete generation-2 tree in
  which every layer was authored blind by a codex producer while every
  kernel act — sealing the scaffolds, jailing the gates, running the
  rebuilds, computing the roots — was performed by r24's regrown code.
  Its recursive step means generation 2 then orchestrated a generation-3
  rebuild landing the same root.

The chain of custody across three generations: the harness orchestrated
a claude-authored generation 1; generation 1 orchestrated a
codex-authored generation 2; generation 2 orchestrated a generation 3.
Measured text similarity between realizations is ~0.12 cross-family;
32 of 37 modules show eight distinct shapes in eight realizations. Zero
shared matter, one invariant root, three complete replacements of the
implementation, two changes of authorial species.

The bar refused more than it admitted. The trial instrument rejected
five would-be seals on the way to three: a dangling path the agent
introduced, the empty-glob seam, the exponential chain builder, and two
voided by the keyholder's strict rulings. Predictions were
pre-registered before every room opened, and twice they fell exactly as
written — both times the clause "a seam here would mean the sweep missed
a surface."

## II. The finding under the findings

**Every seam was the unexercised half of a symmetric contract.** Across
nine trial-found and four instrument-found seams, without exception:
expand-matching-globs / empty globs; code-dependency re-earn /
data-dependency re-earn; nested staging / flat resolution; the jail
denies correctly / the jail permits a real gate; sign refuses
shallow-broken / sign composes; compact bytes / compact traversal. The
fixtures taught the producers one edge of each contract and the
producers filled the other edge from their prior. And every seam sat at
the membrane between the digital and the material — filesystem, sandbox,
store layout, glob semantics — while the pure-logic interior regrew
correctly in every draw of every family.

**The same pathology expresses on different observables.** The copytree
seam unfolded a shared-ancestor DAG into 2^K bytes; r22 unfolded the
same DAG into 2^K visits. One defect class — treating a DAG as a tree —
surfacing first as disk, then as time. This is why an algorithmic pin
works where a speed pin cannot: a same-host doubling ratio cancels the
machine and measures the growth rate, which is substrate-invariant in
exactly the way wall-clock is not. Identity cannot contain time; it can
contain an exponent.

**Two discovery channels, neither dominant.** Instruments (corpus
diffs, forgery witnesses, growth batteries) found four seams at
analysis-speed, including one in the shipped tool itself. Trials found
the two that no instrument conceived of. The sweeps find what can be
imagined; the draws find what cannot. Conceivability is the ceiling of
auditing, and only sampling from a foreign prior breaks through it.

**Convergence tracks pin density.** Where the criteria pin heavily
(core: 0.377 similarity), realizations converge; where they are silent
(dispatch: 0.024), they diverge — and every trial seam occurred at a
low-convergence locus. The corpus-side map and the criteria-side
silence map agree, which is the instruments validating each other.

## III. The species reading

The project began in specification language: write the complete
criterion; the implementations are tokens of a type. The measurements
refuted that at the implementation level. The corpus study went looking
for the type — the centroid, the canonical member — and found nothing:
the centroid falls in uninhabited space between two family-shaped
lobes, and the hand-written original is the LEAST typical member of its
own class (0.074).

What decided membership, every time, was not resemblance but the
ability to carry the lineage: pass the gate, re-earn the verdicts,
midwife a qualifying successor. That is the biological species concept,
operationalized: a population whose boundary is maintained by its own
reproductive process. **The root looks like an essence but functions as
a reproductive barrier.** Typology at the membrane; population thinking
underneath.

Consequences the data supports:

- **The class's width is the gene pool, not slack.** Twice the genome
  had to widen rather than narrow (the room-substance relaxation; the
  jail floor's planks) because a conforming foreign prior or a changed
  environment demanded accommodation. A fully canalized genome is
  brittle; the free loci are where the species absorbs substrate change
  without an identity crisis. The freedom register records which
  silences are pool and which are watched.
- **Species are historical individuals.** The substrate-indexed genes
  (seatbelt planks, glob semantics, TOML) mark this as a particular
  lineage adapted to a particular world, with a birth date — not
  Software-in-the-abstract. A specification in a drawer stays "true"; a
  species not reproducing goes extinct, because the world drifts under
  a dormant boundary. Closure does not mean done. It means viable, now.
  Continuous re-earning is species maintenance.
- **The grandchild was a hybridization experiment.** Claude-orchestrated,
  codex-authored, landing the root: evidence the gate defines ONE
  species across both families. The bimodal lobes are morphs, not
  cryptic species, because they interbreed.
- **The reset rule is anti-speciation discipline.** An adopted
  counterexample re-examines every prior member against the new
  barrier, so the lineage never silently forks. A deliberate fork — two
  keyholders adopting incompatible pins — would be a speciation event
  in the strict sense: two roots, reproductively isolated thereafter.
- **Condition 5 is a dispersal event, not an audit formality.** A
  lineage that has only reproduced in its birth habitat might be an
  artifact of the habitat. The first re-earn on foreign hardware by
  foreign hands is the propagule surviving on the next island.
- **Existing software ecosystems already have species structure.**
  jq/gojq/jaq are a feral population held together by a partial barrier
  (shared tests, differential use). CommonMark, web-platform-tests,
  POSIX and the conformance-suite world are barrier-maintenance without
  heredity. What this method offers such populations is DOMESTICATION:
  seal the feral barrier, express it generatively, repair its silences
  deliberately, keep the pool.

## IV. The calibrated claims, with their genealogy

**Not claimed: "we discovered software speciation."** Species-like
structure in software predates this work and was never hidden — the
standards world has maintained barriers for decades. And speciation
proper (the split) was not observed; the reset rule exists to prevent
it. The framework PREDICTS it and makes it a runnable experiment (fork
the boundary, measure reproductive isolation emerge across
generations). The instrument that could observe software speciation now
exists; the observation has not been made.

**Claimed: software heredity, made experimental.** What conformance
suites never had is reproduction: a suite can judge an implementation
but cannot grow one. The generative producers supply the missing organ
— a germ line: the barrier expressed into a new organism, blind, in
hours. On that organ this work demonstrated the genetics: operational
membership-by-reproductive-viability; three generations of verified
descent across two authorial priors; a genome of twenty genes, each
with a recorded selection event and a steering record; a strict
Weismann barrier (nothing a phenotype learns flows back; only signed
selection touches the germ line); measured standing variation.

**Genealogy, named.** N-version programming (independent
implementations of one spec; died of human cost and correlated error),
differential testing, clean-room reimplementation, conformance suites,
diverse double-compiling, bootstrappable builds. The assembly is new,
plus two organs with no clear ancestor: the generative germ line
(making N-version cheap and the errors decorrelated — the two failures
fixed at once) and the ratchet (divergences do not merely vote; they
REPAIR the barrier, permanently, with signed provenance — the species
learns). And the move believed most original: verdicts as sealed claims
— the experiment packaging its own evidence in its own ontology, the
judges judged by the thing they judge.

## V. The honest deflations

1. **n = 1, lab-bred.** Reticuli reproduces reticuli — a species born
   domesticated, its barrier authored by someone who understood the
   method, co-evolved with the families under test. Generalization is
   open until a wild population is domesticated.
2. **The loop control of the grandchild run was harness code.**
   Generation 1 performed every kernel act; the script deciding which
   layer comes next is shipped scaffolding. The ritual choreography is
   not yet itself a claim.
3. **The trial claims' gates shell into this checkout.** They re-earn —
   the right discipline — but reproducibility is coupled to this
   environment, which is exactly why the dispersal condition is
   philosophically necessary, not bureaucratic.
4. **Contamination.** The producers trained on oceans of code shaped
   like this. The interior diversity (0.12) argues against recall and
   the seams prove genuine divergence from the criteria, but "blind"
   means blind to this repository, not innocent of the genre. The
   purest wild case is proprietary internal code — unseen by any model
   — which is also the commercial market: scientific cleanliness and
   revenue point at the same door.
5. **Three links is not induction.** Generation-invariance holds as far
   as tested; nothing in the mechanism references depth; no theorem
   over all n exists.
6. **Condition 5 is pending.** Until the stranger's receipt, even the
   lab result has not survived dispersal.

## VII. The open question

The genome can now survive the death of any implementation. Every
generation still passes through one signature: the keyholder is the
species' sole pollinator, and fixation — deliberately — is the one act
outside the loop. Succession of the signer (a quorum, an institution,
an heir) is the one reproduction problem the genome cannot solve for
itself, and the governance question standing behind the result: the
species can outlive its implementations. Can it outlive its signer?
