## The new seam: the room recipe written as JSON

At format 3+ a rebuild room receives the preimage recipe rather than
the raw file. The original rewrites it AS TOML (`_dump_recipe`); the
r12 kernel wrote the preimage's canonical JSON serialization into the
file named `reticuli.toml` — and then its own `load_recipe` refused
the file it had just written. Self-incompatible at format 3 and above:
it cannot rebuild, and cannot audit, any claim of the migrated era,
including every layer of the chain and the repository itself. One bug,
three manifestations in the judgment (the substituted gate at 0.4 min,
gen-2, audit_repo — the last a regression against r10 only in the
sense that r10's materialization happened to be correct).

Why no criterion saw it: **every rebuild fixture in the boundary is
format 1**, where the room's recipe is copied byte-for-byte and the
rewrite path never runs. Fifth consecutive seam of the same species —
the unexercised half of a symmetric contract (copy below format 3,
rewrite at and above it). Staged:
`research/proposals/the-room-recipe-is-toml.md`.

Observed beside it, not fully diagnosed and so not proposed: the r12
kernel invokes the producer once PER PRODUCE STEP where the original
invokes once per rebuild and lets the producer iterate. The bundled
codex producer exits 1 under per-step invocation on multi-output
claims. Whether invocation cardinality is contract or freedom is a
register question once the primary seam is pinned and a clean
measurement is possible.
