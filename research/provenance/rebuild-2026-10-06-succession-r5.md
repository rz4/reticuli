## Reading

judges but cannot procreate; r5 shows a tree that procreates but whose
judging and authoring differ from the original in exactly three
unpinned places — and every one of the three was found by machinery

the keyholder is doing the deciding; the implementations keep being
destroyed. The count being zero is not failure — it is the measured
statement that the ratchet has not yet run out of seams, which is the
only honest precondition for the count ever meaning something.
