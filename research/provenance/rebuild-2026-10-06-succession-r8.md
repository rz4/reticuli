## The one seam: the producer is not told what to write

r8's `_produce` sets `RETICULI_OUTPUT` **only when a claim has exactly
one generated output**, and sets it relative rather than absolute. The
original sets it whenever there is a target, absolute. So on any
multi-output claim — which is every layer of this repository's chain —
r8 hands a producer no target, and the bundled producers die on a
KeyError before reaching the model. Two witnesses in one run: the
judge's generation-2 core rebuild (three outputs) and the condition-4
CLI trial on the same claim.

Every pinned producer fixture in the boundary happens to have exactly
one generated output, and `core_check` pins only the variable's
spelling. A kernel can therefore satisfy the entire boundary and be
unable to drive any real producer. Staged:
`research/proposals/name-the-next-output-always.md` — the fourth plank
of the producer-environment contract, after the jail and HOME.

`kernel_check`'s kill-promptness assertion, tightened yesterday from

nested context (audit room, in a sandbox, running the chain build,
whose rebuilds carry a 1-second ceiling). The kill happens; the

So this refusal is not an r8 finding. It is yesterday's pin, too tight
where the previous version was too loose — the r7 complaint in mirror
image. Staged:
`research/proposals/measure-the-kill-against-the-runaway.md` (bound the
refusal against the runaway's 30 seconds, not the ceiling's one). The
research harness's own step caps, which were still calibrated to the
old half-hour window, were raised to 5400 s in the same pass — that is
tooling, not identity.
