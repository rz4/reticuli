## Click one — a declared input may be a pattern

`authoring_check` now passes a PATTERN for `inputs` and asserts the
recipe's declared inputs are the expanded file list. `generated` and
`inputs` are a symmetric pair of pattern lists; every fixture here had
passed a pattern for the first and a literal filename for the second,
so the expansion was exercised on one side only — and the second
family's first tree implemented exactly that side, passing
`inputs=["checks/*.py"]` through verbatim until sealing refused to hash
a file by that name. The self-claim chain is built with precisely that
call, so the unexercised half was the half the chain needs.

Five consecutive codex generations could not find this. One generation
of a different prior did, immediately.

## Click two — the other half of the verdict vocabulary

`surface_check` already forbade `broken` where `failed` belongs; it
said nothing about what identity damage must be called. Ten conforming
implementations produced SIX words for a claim whose pinned input was
changed after sealing (`broken`, `earned`, `error`, `failed`,
`identity mismatch`, `mismatch`), and trial 1 drew the pinned one by
luck rather than by criterion. A recipient reads that word to decide
whether the BYTES are untrustworthy or the CRITERIA refused — opposite
responses — so both directions are pinned now, on a fixture sealed
clean for the purpose.

Writing it turned up a third question the pin deliberately does not
answer: when a claim is damaged AND a gate fails, the original says
`failed`. Whether identity damage should outrank a failing gate is now
an OPEN register item, found because the fixture's first draft reused
a claim that already had a broken gate — the instrument measuring
precedence while meaning to measure vocabulary.

## The reset, deliberately

Adopting these returns the closure count from **1 to 0**. Trial 1 (r9)
remains a conforming member and its measurements stand; what does not
stand is the claim that it qualified against a COMPLETE boundary,
because trial 2 then found a load-bearing hole. The bar's reset rule
exists for exactly this, and paying it is what will make the eventual
three mean something. The alternative — keeping a count of 1 against a
boundary known to have a hole — would make the published bar worth
less than the work that produced it.
