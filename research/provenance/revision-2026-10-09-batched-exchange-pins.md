## Pin 1 — the chain resolves flat

Selected by r15 (claude) and r18 (codex): two independently written
walkers, the same wrong assumption (a component's components reachable
from the component's claim), each walking 2 of the 18 flat-staged chain
layers and reporting the next unresolved. The existing transitivity
fixture carried basecode both flat AND nested, so nesting walkers passed
it. The fixture now removes the nested copy mid-battery and requires the

landing: the standalone form passes the shipped walker and bites both
r15's and r18's.

## Pin 2 — a declared data dependency re-earns its content match

Selected by the corpus performance assay — the first seam selected by an
instrument rather than a trial. A pulled data dependency
(detect_components' shape: a pinned input content-matched to a
component's output, no `from` step) was checked ONCE at seal time and

nothing; with the fix it is refused exactly at the deep audit while the
dependent's own blind gate stays green; 104 tests and the full gate hold.
