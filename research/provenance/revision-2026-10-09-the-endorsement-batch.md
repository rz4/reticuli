## Pin 1 — an endorsement composes

`attest.sign` signs the CHAIN root (`sign_root` folds every declared
component into signed identity) but re-earned only the claim's own
gates. Witness, measured before landing: a composed claim whose
component code was forged — its own gate blind by construction, the
deep audit refusing — was SIGNED. The strongest verdict in the system
quietly carried the shallow result across the composed boundary.

The fix: on a claim that declares components, `sign` re-earns the
COMPOSED verdict (`audit_deep`) before the ceremony, refusing with the
broken layers named. For a leaf claim the audits coincide and nothing
changes. The fixture signs the data-dependency witness both ways: the
forged chain is refused with "composed" in the reason; the restored
chain signs. Scope note, decided deliberately: `record.emit` keeps its
own-gates semantics — its contract is explicitly per-claim ("a failing
gate is stated, never refused"), so a record states and a signature
endorses; only the endorsement composes.
