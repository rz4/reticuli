# Bounded investigator policy evolution: an instrument test

2026-10-13. Local, standard-library execution; no producer calls or model-weight
changes. Product base: `4c5ca3824c252a72665693d6e808af9a662f61c8`, with the
`research/harness/improvement/` patch. This is an instrument result over public
controlled fixtures, not a claim of general recursive capability improvement.

## Procedure

```bash
python3 research/harness/improvement/evolve.py run \
  --out /tmp/investigator-lineage --rounds 8 --seeds 2 --probes 8
python3 research/harness/improvement/evolve.py replay /tmp/investigator-lineage
```

The initial investigator repeats disclosed gate examples. The active investigator
proposes unused mutation operations as extensions to its inherited policy, using
previous training search results to order proposals. The controller evaluates
all proposals with identical per-run budgets, chooses by training recall, and
tests the winner on a separate public sibling-realization panel and seed range.
Both training and validation need an absolute gain of at least 0.05; validation
target regression or a candidate run failure blocks local adoption. A successor
is compiled from the parent's source with its policy extended by one operation.
It becomes the program proposing the following generation.

Training: mixed config, base64, and query realization pools. Validation: shared
config, base64, and query pools, plus a compliant config control. The pools share
underlying behavioral tasks; this is not unseen software or a secret benchmark.

## What this establishes

The implementation can automatically generate, select, inherit, and replay a
sequence of increasingly effective probe policies under fixed per-run limits.
Its failure and stopping conditions are executable. Human edits between the
seven adopted generations were not needed.

The operation library, compiler, proposal algorithm, and fixture semantics were
authored in advance. The learner searches that finite library and does not invent
algorithms or repair its evaluator. The initial policy is deliberately weak;
the result does not compare against a strong evolutionary-search baseline,
demonstrate a recursive advantage over frozen successor generation, or establish
transfer to independently developed software. The sibling validation panel is
public and reused, so it cannot support a confirmatory generalization claim.
All execution was local and external promotion remains incomplete.

## Reproduced 2026-10-14, with one defect and two sharper limits

Re-run on a second host, arm64 Darwin 23.5.0 with python 3.14.3, from the same
commands. The table above reproduced exactly: seven adopted generations in the
order duplicate, digits, empty, junk, plus, truncate, whitespace; final recall
1.0 against a frozen 0.0; 356 evaluation runs and 6,304 realization calls;
stop reason `mutation vocabulary exhausted`. Replay re-earned through both the
working tree and the retained snapshot, and the gate wrote `LINEAGE_OK`. The
calibration campaign reproduced as well, at 1.0 against 0.0 over 70 runs with
900 realization calls in each arm and no failed runs.

One defect, now fixed. `campaign.stop` caught `ProcessLookupError` alone. A
candidate that exits on its own leaves a process group whose only member is its
unreaped leader, and signalling that group reports `ESRCH` on Linux but `EPERM`
on macOS. The unhandled `PermissionError` escaped the `finally` that was meant
to clean up, so on a declared platform a crashing candidate aborted the
controller instead of being recorded as a failed run, and leaked its pipes on
the way out. `tests/test_improvement.py` caught it on the first run. The same
path is reached from `evolve.request_proposals`, where the proposer prints its
reply and exits immediately, which made a lineage search intermittently fail
rather than reliably fail. The handler now treats both errnos as nothing left
to signal and waits and closes regardless.

Two limits the prose above understated, both verified against this run:

The recall ceiling is a property of the vocabulary. The seven operations are an
exact minimal cover of the eight planted omissions, `duplicate` covering one per
family, with nothing left over and no operation that finds nothing. Every
operation earns a positive gain, so every one is adopted, and recall arrives at
1.0 in the round the vocabulary runs out. The monotone ladder counts a cover
being enumerated. Selection never rejects a proposal on its merits.

The second panel is not independent. Its targets carry the same omission labels
as the training targets and differ only in the realization pool, so the two
columns agree to four decimal places at all seven generations. It checks that
the same probes still find the same obligations when implementations agree with
each other, which is worth checking, but it cannot detect a policy fitted to
this label set.

Separately, two independent runs produce identical policies, decisions and
scores, and different evidence roots, because each run record retains its own
`elapsed_seconds` and standard-error excerpt. Stripping those two fields makes
the lineage content byte-identical across runs, so the root currently names a
transcript where it could name the result.
