## Why the root moved

six-row table of a 2026-09-era decomposition; `criteria/self_check.py`
opened with "six layered claims" and promised "the six roots are exactly
the values pinned below." The chain has been twenty claims since the
final bundle, and the lockfile pins twenty roots. These are pinned
files — the normative surface — and a stranger reading the room would
have been told, by the boundary itself, a wrong count and a wrong
structure.

`docs/transitions.md` says a moved root is never incidental cleanup,
and this is not: the change is to what the pinned surface *asserts*
about the claim. Five corrections, no behavior touched: twenty claims,
nineteen chained plus the standalone reference (with `LAYERS` named as
the authoritative list), twenty nested copies, a nineteen-deep chain,
twenty roots.

## What moved

Only the repository root: neither file is a layer's acceptance check,
so all twenty layer roots held — which is itself the lockfile doctrine
working as stated ("change what a layer is checked for and they move;
change anything else and they hold"). Gate green cold at the new root,
audit receipt re-earned, room refreshed.
