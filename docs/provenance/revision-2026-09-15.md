# The v2.1 revision: closing seven measured under-specifications

    kernel claim  d64cc301082f…  →  4b90feef318d…

A day of building on the kernel produced eleven recorded places where the
acceptance suite left a conforming kernel free to behave differently. Four of
them were not academic: one killed the cost envelope's last-resort unit, one
made a crosscheck flaky, one broke self-hosting outright, and on two of them
the **two independent blind rebuilds visibly disagreed**. This revision pins
the seven that should not have been free, and deliberately leaves the rest.

## What is now pinned

| # | behavior | why it earned a pin |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | a kernel that APPLIES a sandbox must set `RETICULI_JAILED` for the wrapped process | the suite pinned only the receiving half; a gate that itself runs claims died with `sandbox_apply: Operation not permitted`. **The two rebuilds split on this** |
| 2 | a sandboxed gate gets a writable `TMPDIR`/`HOME` | a real sandbox permits writes only inside the claim, so an inherited temp dir is a path the gate is forbidden to use |
| 3 | `cost()` totals wall-clock, and never accepts `seconds` from a producer's self-report | v1 totalled it; the kernel's own check never said so, so the rebuild dropped it |
| 4 | the cost envelope compares exactly ONE unit — the strongest shared (usd > tokens > calls > seconds) | comparing every shared unit let two 11ms builds be gated on wall-clock; it failed CI on ubuntu 3.11 |
| 5 | `seal()` returns the manifest | **the two rebuilds split on this** (one returned `None`) |
| 6 | `rebuild` refuses a target that already holds bytes | v1 allowed resuming; v2 refuses. Pinned so it is a decision, not an accident |
| 7 | the passing gate status string is `"ok"` | failure classes were pinned; the success spelling was not, making results incomparable across kernels |

Still deliberately free: store/ledger/signature filenames, the manifest schema
beyond `name`/`root`, `sandbox()`'s result shape, the `gate_deciders`
heuristic, mutation-survivor representation, default timeout and tolerance
ranges, and the kernel's public surface beyond the pinned symbols. Those are
implementation choices; pinning them would constrain without protecting.

## Evidence the revision has content

Every new assertion was **bite-tested**: the behavior was removed from a
working kernel, the suite was shown to fail naming that assertion, and the
kernel restored. Two were re-verified independently afterwards.

More telling: the **original machine-regrown kernel fails the revised suite**,
on pins 1, 2, 3 and 4 in that order — exactly the four behaviors fixed during
the day's work. Pins 5, 6 and 7 it happened to satisfy without ever promising
to; the evidence that they needed pinning came from the *other* vendor's
rebuild.

## What happened to the proven claim

`d64cc301…` was not overwritten. It is preserved, sealed and intact, at
[`conformance/kernel-2.0/`](birth/) — with the machine-regrown bytes exactly as
they landed and its three-machine proof still recorded on its manifest. It
verifies there unchanged, because a root covers claim-relative paths and
moving a claim does not rename it.

This matters for honesty: the revised claim could not simply be earned in
place. The birth record's kernel *predates* the fixes and fails four of the
new pins. Editing the suite under it would have forced overwriting the
regrown bytes with today's kernel and quietly destroyed the one artifact that
makes the bootstrap story checkable.

## What the revision costs

**The three-machine proof does not transfer.** It was earned against the old
suite, and the new claim is a different claim. `4b90feef…` is `sealed` with no
proof; re-earning it requires fresh blind rebuilds against the revised suite,
cross-vendor, and a new crosscheck. Until then the strongest statement about
the revised claim is that the living kernel earns its gate.

Identity was re-measured across CPython 3.11, 3.13 and 3.14: both roots are
interpreter-independent, as before.

Only the kernel layer of the self-hosting chain moved
(`examples/self/`); the five layers above it are unchanged, because a layer's
root covers its own check and not its component's — the property that
document describes, observed rather than assumed.
